# Sonic Unleashed Recompiled × Archipelago — V0.9 Beta

**Release:** [V0.9](https://github.com/ZMGBZak/Sonic-Unleashed-Recompiled-Archipelago-Updates/releases/tag/V0.9)

## New in V0.9

- Progressive Stats checks: configurable upgrades enter the Archipelago item/check pool, with an F8 stat display when enabled.
- Shops checks: roughly 164 additional checks from ordinary in-game shops; special traveling merchant coverage is limited.
- Exorcism checks: 11 camera missions; the first tutorial is excluded.
- VHS location checks.
- Collectibles Radar for nearby CD, VHS and Art Books, independent of the Medal Radar, configurable from F8.
- Slot-machine 777 probability adjustments.

## Fixes

- Goal Auto Collect protection: receiving items from other players must not falsely satisfy your own victory goal.
- Additional fixes and adjustments.

## Downloads

The release contains Windows, Linux and APWorld packages. Their exact filenames, sizes and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [updates/manifest.json](../updates/manifest.json).
